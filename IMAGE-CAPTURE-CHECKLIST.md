# Winds of Valen wiki image capture checklist

Last audited: 3 September 2026

The installed game containers and the wiki's existing image archive have been checked. Of 541 wiki entries, 474 now have a primary image. Every referenced image file exists locally. The 67 entries below do not have a safe, exact image match in the packed files or existing archive and need an in-game capture.

## Capture guidelines

- NPCs and creatures: capture the whole character at a readable distance, preferably from the front or a slight angle, without dialogue panels covering them.
- Locations: take a wide establishing screenshot that makes the area recognisable.
- Resource nodes: show the complete mineable rock at close range and, if possible, keep its interaction label visible.
- Items: capture the inventory icon and exact in-game name. `Volcanic Gold Ore` should be verified first because no matching item icon exists in the current packed item-icon set.
- Use the suggested wiki slug as the filename, for example `agnes-treadwell.png`. Screenshots can be supplied in PNG, WebP, or JPEG format.
